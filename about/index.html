Short bio

Hetvi Makwana (@hetvimakwanaa) is a Mumbai comedy creator known for desi relatable POV skits: the face that goes from full hype to deadpan in one beat. Managed by @cretoro_.

Long bio

Hetvi Makwana is a Mumbai-based Gujarati comedy creator. Her reels turn everyday disasters into one-take jokes: the deflation, the held stare and the moment she cracks up at herself, shot on Mumbai's streets, local trains and food stalls, or in a parked car. Her most-liked reel, “Dating in 2026 be like”, has 1,258,145 likes, and 26 of her reels have passed 100,000 likes (as of 7 Nov 2026). She has made reels with brands including Maybelline, Dove, Parachute Advansed, McDonald's India and Lancôme. She is managed by @cretoro_.
